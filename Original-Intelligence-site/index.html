The explorative approach
AI should not be the destination of thinking.
It should become part of a cycle of exploration.
Don’t start with AI.
Start with curiosity.
The learner enters the process before the machine does.
The human check
What it is
A five-point check for any AI-supported activity. Each letter of HUMAN asks whether an essential part of human thinking is still present.
Why it’s needed
AI can complete a task without the learner doing the thinking, and an answer can feel like understanding. The Human Check makes sure AI amplifies the learner’s judgement and agency instead of quietly replacing them.
If a box can’t be ticked, redesign the activity.
H
Human judgement
What decisions must remain human?
U
Understanding
Does the learner understand what AI is doing?
M
Metacognition
Is the learner thinking about their own thinking?
A
Agency
Who is actually making the decisions?
N
New thinking
Is AI helping the learner think further?
Two questions. One design principle.
01 · Explorative approach
Where is the learner in the thinking cycle?
02 · Human check
Are judgement, understanding, metacognition, agency and new thinking still present?
AI should increase the quality of human thinking, not merely the speed of task completion.

Exploration
Concept & framework developed and created by Genesco Dela Cruz
01 · Exploration layer
Learners move between content, questions, building, research, notes and peers towards a goal, product or skill.
02 · AI layer
AI is part of the cycle, so we explore in greater depth: sparking curiosity, co-teaching content, AI-integrated projects, guided research, smart summaries and questions that guide discussion.
03 · Teacher layer
As AI supports students and teachers, we grow as educators: coach, curator, mentor, and an ethical, social and cultural compass who develops critical thinking and human skills.
